### Overview
Travis is a postdoctoral fellow in the Department of Internal Medicine, Division of Nephrology, and
a recent addition to the Pezzolesi Lab. He is also supported by a Cardiovascular T32 training grant
through the University of Utah’s CVRTI. He earned his Ph.D. in Human Genetics from the
University of Utah, where his research focused on characterizing genetic modifiers in the rare
disorder NGLY1 deficiency. His current work applies computational and statistical genomics to
investigate how genetic variation contributes to the disproportionate burden of cardiovascular,
metabolic, and kidney diseases in Pacific Islander populations. Outside of the lab, Travis enjoys
spending time with his wife and four children.
