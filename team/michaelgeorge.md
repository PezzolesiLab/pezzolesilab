### Overview
Michael is an undergraduate student at the University of Utah, studying Biology with an emphasis in Genetic and Genomics. His current research focuses on RNA sequencing data from Native Hawaiians and Pacific Islanders, along with research into Denisovan variants that appear in that population. He loves reading, performing and teaching music, and hanging out with his family.
